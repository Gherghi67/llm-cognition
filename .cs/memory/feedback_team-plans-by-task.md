On 2026-11-14, actor bogdan-gherghina-erepubliklabs-com asked for a team task plan for the transformer-layers project. He corrected my per-person owner split: "do not split on people, just split on tasks as a team".

**How to apply:** in plans for this team, use task, due date and status columns, and no Owner column. Don't ask who owns what. See [[course-project-transformer-layers]].
