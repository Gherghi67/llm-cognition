What to submit by 14 Nov

A topic and a preliminary plan covering:

  • related work and papers on the subject
  • available data, its size, and a first exploratory analysis
  • modelling strategies and evaluation metrics you can afford with your compute
  • expected results and evaluation (qualitative, automatic, model comparisons)
  • open questions and bottlenecks

If the paper needs compute you don't have, say so as a limitation and reproduce the effect on smaller models that run on Google Colab.

Final deliverables

  • Report of 4–8 pages in the ACL Overleaf template, not anonymous
  • A Contributions section after the Conclusions and before the Bibliography, stating what each member did
  • An LLM disclosure section: which models and versions you used, how, and the prompts
  • Implementation code and a slide presentation

The instructors ask you not to write the report with LLMs, and unclear writing can cost points even when the technical work is sound. They recommend this post.
